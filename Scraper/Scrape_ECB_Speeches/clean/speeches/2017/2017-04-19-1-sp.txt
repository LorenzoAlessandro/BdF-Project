European Monetary and Financial Outlook (Slides from the presentation)

Presentation by Peter Praet, Member of the Executive Board of the ECB, 26th Annual Hyman P. Minsky Conference on the State of the US and World Economies, 19 April 2017

Executive Board member Peter Praet discusses the European monetary and financial outlook at the Levy Economics Institute's 26th Annual Hyman P. Minsky Conference in New York. He looks at steps taken to restore resilience and to achieve a European banking union.
